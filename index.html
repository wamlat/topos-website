TOPOS

Spaces, maps and local structure

The eye is the central moving image. Many small wing, ring, and eye fragments circulate through the photographic grain and react to nearby fragments. Their motion gradually forms one shared field around the pupil. A conflicting region remains unsettled before the image draws together again. Handwritten category theory, topology, geometry, and sheaf diagrams are exposed around the iris and grow clearer during registration. Scrolling changes the sequence while the field remains in motion.
